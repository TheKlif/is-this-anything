Is This Anything?

A whole city is built on sea stacks. Hundreds of them, big red sandstone pillars standing up out of the ocean, and nearly every substantial single one has a single building on top. Little half-timbered houses, a pub with a thatched roof, some city buildings with slate roofs, etc.

In the middle, one of the ocean stacks is way wider than the rest. The top of it is a flat round cobblestone plaza, acting as a town square.
