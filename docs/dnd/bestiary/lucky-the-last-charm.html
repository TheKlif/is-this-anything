Lucky The Last Charm - Is This Anything?

Meet Lucky.

Lucky is an ancient archfey trickster bound to a cursed artifact known as the Lucky Charms, which consists of seven magical tokens. For centuries, mortals repeatedly captured him and stole his magic piecemeal. The modern image of a jovial cereal mascot is a degraded folk memory of this older entity. Each charm represents a fragment of his true name, split apart by the original binding ritual. Having finally reclaimed all seven tokens, he is whole for the first time in millennia. He is not evil in a cosmic sense, but his prolonged humiliation has left him furious and eager to end the game.

The centuries of being hunted have made him manic, theatrical, and unhinged. He initiates conflicts with theatrical flair before turning completely cold. His expressions of joy are authentic, but they mask profound murderous intent. He references his own mascot iconography with the bitterness of someone weaponizing an insult. He is no longer fleeing and actively seeks confrontation.

Upon encountering adversaries, he spreads his arms wide, allowing the seven tokens to orbit him. Presenting a smile composed entirely of teeth, he recites his opening monologue. He lists hearts, starts, horseshoes, clovers, blue moons, pots of gold, rainbows... and then he stops, looks directly at his targets without changing his expression... "AND YOU'RE ALL GONNA DIE!

Lucky resides in a collapsed, rainbow pocket dimension that is both beautiful and rotting. The environment features optional gravity, color that bleeds away from surfaces near his true form, and failed escape routes that loop directly back to the entrance. In combat, he functions as a trickster controller utilizing escalating chaos and burst damage. His legendary actions and passive auras are tied to the seven tokens. As he loses health, more of these charms activate. Adversaries can attempt to weaken him by using a high-difficulty contested action to catch and attune to individual tokens.

Attacks directed at him force a luck save. Failures on this save grant him advantage on his subsequent turn. He also utilizes a lair action called Pot of Gold, which teleports him and blinds targets with a burst of golden light. Resolving the encounter permanently requires bargaining with him by acknowledging the abuse he suffered. The true conclusion involves breaking the binding ritual rather than defeating him in combat. Destroying his physical form without releasing the binding merely resets the cycle, guaranteeing that another individual will eventually find the box.

The Seven Tokens. Lucky is bound to seven floating magical fragments representing his true name. At the start of combat, all seven tokens are active. As Lucky's hit points cross specific thresholds, tokens shatter or hyper-charge, changing his auras and available legendary actions. While active, each token grants a passive benefit:

  • Hearts: Allies within 30 feet have advantage on death saving throws. Lucky gains temporary hit points equal to damage dealt by legendary actions.
  • Stars: Lucky can cast Misty Step as a bonus action without expending a spell slot.
  • Horseshoes: Ranged attack rolls against Lucky have disadvantage.
  • Clovers: Lucky adds a +2 bonus to all saving throws.
  • Blue Moons: Dim light within 60 feet of Lucky becomes magical darkness for enemies.
  • Pots of Gold: When Lucky hits with a spell or attack, he deals an extra 14 (4d6) radiant damage, manifesting as a blinding flash of golden coins.
  • Rainbows: The ground within 30 feet of Lucky is treated as difficult terrain for enemies.

Luck Drain. When a creature hits Lucky with a melee attack, that creature must make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, Lucky absorbs a fraction of their fortune; he gains advantage on his next attack roll or saving throw.

Legendary Resistance (4/Day). If Lucky fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

Magic Resistance. Lucky has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

Actions

Multiattack. Lucky makes three attacks: two with his Spectral Cereal Shards and one with his Fey Step Strike.

Spectral Cereal Shards. Melee or Ranged Spell Attack: +13 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 6) force damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

Fey Step Strike. Lucky teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see and makes one melee weapon attack (+12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target). Hit: 18 (3d6 + 6) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

Catch a Token (Recharge 5-6). A creature within 10 feet that can see Lucky can attempt to snatch one of his orbiting tokens. The creature must use its action to make a contested Dexterity (Acrobatics) check against Lucky's spell save DC (21). If the creature succeeds, the token is pulled away, deactivating that token's passive aura and legendary action until the end of Lucky's next turn, after which it returns to orbit.

Bonus Actions

Prismatic Leap. Lucky moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks, leaving a trail of shimmering, acidic residue in his wake. Any creature that enters or ends its turn in that space takes 10 (3d6) acid damage.

Reactions

Uncanny Parrying. When a creature Lucky can see hits him with an attack, Lucky adds 6 to his Armor Class against that attack.

Legendary Actions

Lucky can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Lucky regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

  • Token Pulse. Lucky channels the active energy of his tokens, forcing all creatures within 20 feet to make a DC 21 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
  • Glimmering Shift (Costs 2 Actions). Lucky forces a target he can see to make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the target is teleported up to 30 feet to a space of Lucky's choice and rendered incapacitated until the end of its next turn.
  • Shattered Memory (Costs 3 Actions). Lucky invokes the collective trauma of his millennia of imprisonment. Each enemy within 60 feet must make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
