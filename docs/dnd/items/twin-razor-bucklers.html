Twin Razor Bucklers - Is This Anything?

Twin Razor Bucklers
Armor (Shield) and Weapon (Martial Melee), Uncommon
Cost: 200 gp
Weight: 6 lbs (pair)

These steel bucklers are held in each hand. The outer circumference of each shield is ground to a sharp edge. Wielding these items occupies both hands, preventing the use of other weapons or the performance of somatic spell components unless a feature explicitly allows it. You must be proficient with both shields and martial weapons to utilize this item without mechanical penalties.

Armor Class Bonus
While wielding both bucklers, you gain a +2 bonus to your Armor Class. You cannot benefit from any other shields while using these items.

Melee Attacks
You can make martial melee weapon attacks with these items. They possess the Light property.

  • Bludgeoning Strike: 5 foot reach. On a hit, it deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This attack relies on mass and force and uses your Strength modifier for attack and damage rolls.
  • Slashing Edge: 5 foot reach. On a hit, it deals 1d6 slashing damage. This attack requires edge alignment and precision. It possesses the Finesse property, allowing you to choose your Strength or Dexterity modifier for attack and damage rolls.

Bonus Action Push
You can use a bonus action to attempt to push a target within 5 feet of you that is no more than one size larger than you. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw. The save DC equals 8 plus your proficiency bonus plus your Strength modifier.

  • Failure: You push the target in a single direction away from you. You move into the spaces the target vacates. This movement uses your available walking speed and is treated as difficult terrain. If this forced movement pins the target against an immovable object or terrain, the target becomes grappled. If the target is not pinned at the end of this movement, it must succeed on a second Dexterity saving throw or fall prone.
  • Success: The target evades the push. You move through the target space using your movement. This movement provokes an opportunity attack from the target.

Defensive Reaction
When you are hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to add a +5 bonus to your Armor Class against that single attack, potentially causing the attack to miss.
